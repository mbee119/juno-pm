## What it demonstrates

_The one flow this prototype proves._
The prototype demonstrates the experience of Juno and shows what Juno’s output looks and feels like in Jira. There are 4 demo scenarios: A. Spiralling thread, B.Healthy ticket, C. Ambigious thread and D. Out of scope Ask
What it shows

Fit: a one-line verdict, ranked risks, and a 350-word cap read cleanly in a narrow Jira comment column.
Light interaction: one @mention, a short wait, and an inline reply.
Visible guardrails: NO RISK, NEEDS CLARIFICATION, and the refusal show Juno staying quiet, asking, or declining when it should.
Checkable claims: clickable citations let you verify any risk in seconds.

What it doesn’t show

Whether Juno’s risk calls are accurate or noisy.
How @Juno triggers inside real Jira.
The time saved. Only a timed run on a real epic can prove that.
